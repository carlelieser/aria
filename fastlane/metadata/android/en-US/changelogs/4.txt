Spotify import is back: connect your account to bring in saved tracks, albums, playlists, and followed artists via a proxy service, since Spotify's official API is no longer reachable from the app directly. Lyrics now actually load and display correctly in the player, with a smoother scroll and legible text in light mode. The album/artist/playlist detail header has been reworked for better contrast in both themes. Also fixes the play button animation, the playlist description layout, and a player performance issue that caused excess re-rendering during playback.
